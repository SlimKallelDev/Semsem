Semsem Pets

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Semsem Pets is a community for people who care for pets. Users can publish posts and communicate through the app. We are committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) across Semsem Pets.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Semsem Pets prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of a child, sextortion, child sex trafficking, and any content or behavior that sexually exploits or endangers a person under 18.

Users must not create, upload, request, store, share, promote, or distribute CSAM or other content that sexually exploits or endangers children. This prohibition applies to posts, images, profiles, messages, and every other feature of Semsem Pets.

How to report a concern

Report a concerning post from its post screen in the Semsem Pets app: open the post actions, choose Report, select Child safety concern, and include any relevant details. Reports are sent to Semsem for review.

Do not download, forward, or upload suspected CSAM as part of a report. If a child may be in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement or the appropriate child protection authority.

Review and enforcement

Semsem reviews child safety reports and may remove reported content, restrict or terminate accounts, and preserve relevant information for lawful requests. We will report apparent CSAM and other suspected child sexual exploitation to the appropriate authorities or designated child safety organizations when required by applicable law.

We may cooperate with law enforcement and child protection authorities in investigations, consistent with applicable law.

Legal compliance

Semsem Pets will comply with applicable child safety laws and respond to valid legal requests through the appropriate process.

Child safety contact

For child safety reports, use the in-app reporting steps above. For official child safety inquiries, use the developer contact email listed on the Semsem Pets Google Play store page.
